# Third-party wordnet data attributions

Generated by tools/wordnet_build.py. This app's semantic-composition
tier (BlissSemanticComposer Stage A) uses word/synset data derived
from the following open wordnets, aggregated via the Open
Multilingual Wordnet project (Bond & Paik 2012; Bond & Foster 2013).
Hypernym relations are derived from Princeton WordNet 3.0.

- MultiWordNet (Italian) — Fondazione Bruno Kessler — CC BY 3.0 — https://multiwordnet.fbk.eu/
- Open English Wordnet, derived from Princeton WordNet 3.0 — CC BY 4.0 — https://wordnet.princeton.edu/
